  *) mod_http3: Less memory per connection. The QUIC connection keeps its
     connection IDs in 16 slots of its own, not in an APR pool (8 KB at
     least), and makes the CONNECTION_CLOSE buffer only when it closes. The
     session and stream allocators keep at most MaxMemFree, as in mod_http2,
     so an idle connection gives back its peak memory.
